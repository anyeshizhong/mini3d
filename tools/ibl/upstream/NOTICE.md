Mini3D adaptation: `tools/ibl/bake.py` supplies desktop OpenGL 3.3 uniforms,
fullscreen triangles, HDR decoding and float framebuffer readback. At shader
compile time it adds `#version 330 core` and removes the ES precision declaration.
It does not change the sampling, distributions, filtering or BRDF integration.
Only Lambertian and GGX are executed; Charlie/sheens are unused. The output
has nine GGX levels with perceptual roughness `level / 8`, avoiding the upstream
JavaScript loop's extra level. Source cube mipmaps support importance sampling;
final specular mips are individually convolved, never generated by box filtering.

The runtime environment sampling in `mini3d/material_renderer.py` is an
independent, small adaptation of classic split-sum equations documented by
Khronos and Filament. It retains Mini3D's direct BRDF and color pipeline.
Full reference discussion: [quality-q1.md](../../../docs/renderer-v2/quality-q1.md).
